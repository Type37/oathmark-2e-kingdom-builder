# Name pools

The dice buttons draw only from names printed in published books the owner has, cited by printed page. Nothing is invented, and every entry was checked against the PDF text of its cited page.

- *Oathmark: Second Edition* (Osprey Games, 2026)
- *Dragon Rampant*, second edition (Osprey Games), by Daniel Mersey. It has no name tables, so these come from its sample warband, photo captions, scenarios and sample-warband chapter.

Left out on purpose: real people named in the book (sculptors, painters, actors) and allusions to other works (Elric, Beowulf, Noggin the Nog, Lady Macbeth, Thane Tostig).
